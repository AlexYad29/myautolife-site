MyAutoLife Privacy Policy

Version dated October 20, 2026

1. Developer and contact details

MyAutoLife (the “App”) is a mobile application for keeping a personal vehicle ownership and maintenance journal.

Developer and contact person for privacy inquiries: Alexander Nikolaevich Yadryshnikov (individual developer, Russia).

Email: myautolifesupport@gmail.com. For questions about personal information, please contact the Developer at the email address above.

The current policy is available in the App and on the MyAutoLife privacy page linked from its app-store listing.

2. How the App handles data

The App works without registration or a user account. The app-store version does not offer cloud synchronization with the Developer's servers. Your vehicle journal is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to the Developer as part of normal journal operation.

Certain components, including Google ML Kit, may send technical information to Google (Section 4). Your operating system may include App data in system backups (Section 6). You may also choose to export or share records with another service.

3. Information stored on your device

Local information may include:

  • vehicle make, model, year, odometer, fuel type and, optionally, the VIN, license plate number and photos;
  • fuel purchases, washes, maintenance, repairs, tires, income, expenses, dates, prices, mileage, locations entered manually, notes, parts and labor items;
  • receipt photos and other image attachments;
  • reminders and their completion history;
  • preferences such as theme, language, currency, notification times and distance/volume units (kilometers or miles; liters, US gallons or Imperial/UK gallons).

This information is used to show your vehicle history, calculate fuel consumption and ownership costs, generate reports and schedule local reminders. Changing language or units does not require transmitting your vehicle journal over the internet.

Records, notes and images may contain personal information if you choose to enter it.

4. Receipt recognition and Google ML Kit technical data

The App uses bundled Google ML Kit models to read QR codes and recognize text from receipts on your device. According to Google's ML Kit terms, image inputs and recognized outputs are processed on-device and are not sent to Google's servers by ML Kit.

ML Kit may contact Google servers for component maintenance and send diagnostic and usage metrics. Such information may include device and App information, a per-installation identifier, performance measurements, API configuration, input/output sizes and error codes. Google processes these metrics under its applicable terms and policies. See https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms and https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.

The version covered by this policy contains no advertising SDKs and no behavioral analytics tools used by the Developer to build user profiles.

5. Device permissions and features

The App uses system camera and photo-selection features when you choose to take or select a photo of a receipt or vehicle. It may request notification permission and use system mechanisms to restore scheduled reminders after a device restart.

On Android, technical permissions may include internet access, network-state access, vibration and boot-completed notifications. Connectivity supports certain Google ML Kit functions; it is not used for vehicle-journal cloud synchronization. The audited version does not request location, contacts or microphone permissions.

6. Backups, exports and user-directed sharing

You may create backups and reports, including CSV, Excel, PDF and MyAutoLife backup files, and select where to save or share them. Depending on the format, exports may contain VINs, license plate numbers, expenses, notes and photos.

When you set a password for a MyAutoLife backup, the file is encrypted using AES-256-GCM and a key derived with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256. The App does not store the password. Without a password, the backup is not encrypted. Protection depends on your password strength.

Any data you choose to send to email, messaging services, cloud drives or other apps is then handled by the recipient service under its own terms and privacy practices.

Operating-system backups. If enabled on your device, Android may include the App database, photos and settings in Google backups and restore them after reinstalling the App or moving to another device. On iOS, App information may be included in iCloud or computer backups. These backups are managed by the operating system and your device settings, not the Developer. The actual backup contents depend on the device settings and App version.

7. Storage periods and deletion

Local journal data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App's data or uninstall the App. The app-store version provides options to delete individual entries, images, reminders and a vehicle with its history, as well as “More → Delete all data”.

Information deleted within the App is removed from the active local journal and excluded from newly created MyAutoLife backups. Previously exported files, operating-system backups and copies held by third-party services may remain and must be managed through those services or your device settings. Restoring an older backup may restore previously deleted records.

The Developer does not hold a server-side copy of your vehicle journal to delete.

8. Contacting the Developer

If you email the Developer about support or privacy, the Developer receives your email address, message and any files you attach. This information is used to respond to your request and comply with applicable legal obligations, and is retained only as long as necessary for those purposes. You may request correction or deletion of correspondence data by using the contact email above, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

9. Children

The App is intended for vehicle recordkeeping and is not designed specifically for children. The Developer does not create children's profiles or ask users to provide their age. Any additional safeguards required for particular age groups under applicable laws or app store policies must be implemented before the App is made available to those audiences.

10. Changes to this policy

Material changes to data practices will be reflected in a revised policy on the privacy page linked from the app-store listing and in “More → About → Privacy”. The revision date appears at the top. This policy will be reviewed before adding accounts, cloud synchronization or new third-party SDKs.
